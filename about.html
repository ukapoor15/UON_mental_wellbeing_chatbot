A bit about Mindbot

Learn more about how and why we built this AI assistant.

Our Mission

Mindbot was created to provide students with instant support for mental health, stress, and general wellbeing. Our goal is to create a safe, confidential, and accessible tool for students to seek guidance whenever they need it.

Development Timeline

🛠 Idea & Research (Jan 2025)

We identified the need for a student mental health chatbot and conducted research on AI-based solutions.

🚀 Development Phase (Feb-Mar 2025)

We built the chatbot using AI models and designed a user-friendly interface.

🎉 Launch & Feedback (Apr 2025)

We launched the chatbot at UON and collected student feedback for future improvements.
